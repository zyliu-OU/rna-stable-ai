# Research-use notice

RNA-StableAI V1 is an experimental research prototype. Descriptive sequence scores and
untrained encoder outputs are not validated predictors of biological stability,
expression, therapeutic performance, or clinical safety. Local development training
and checkpoints remain experimental; biological stability and function are unvalidated.
